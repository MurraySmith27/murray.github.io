DeckTerra

A 4X strategy game built around cards!!

DeckTerra is a 4X deckbuilder where your empire is only as strong as your deck. Explore procedurally generated maps, research new technologies, develop your settlements and raise armies, all while building a deck that fits your strategy.

Genre4X Strategy / Deckbuilder
ReleaseCOMING 2026
PlatformsWindows, Mac, Linux
ModesSingle-player, Online PvP

~ Features ~

  • 🏰 City placement matters. Settle near plenty of resources and a source of fresh water.
  • 🧪 Discover new technology. Work your way through the ages to expand your empire and bolster your military.
  • 🙏 Appease the gods. Devote yourself to one of four deities and spend faith to unlock powerful buffs.
  • 💰 Build a trade network. Send traders out to build relationships with neighbouring kingdoms.
  • 🃏 Your deck shapes your kingdom. Got science cards? Build science buildings. Too many labour cards? Found more cities!
  • 🏆 Many paths to victory: Science, Combat or Religious. No two kingdoms are alike!
